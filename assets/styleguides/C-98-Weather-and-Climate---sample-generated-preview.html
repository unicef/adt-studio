Big Question

What is the atmosphere, and what is weather?

Suppose a friend asked you what the weather is like today. What would you say? How would you know?

You might say it is cold and raining. Weather is what the air outside is like at any one time and place.

Regular Content Page

Water Falls to Earth as Precipitation

Vocabulary

water vapor, n. the gas form of water

precipitation, n. rain, snow, sleet, or hail

One of the gases in our atmosphere is water vapor. When water vapor cools, it may change into tiny droplets of liquid water.

This liquid water may fall from the sky. This is precipitation. Precipitation can take the form of rain, snow, sleet, or hail.

The type of precipitation that falls depends on air temperature. Rain falls when the temperature is above freezing, while snow and sleet form when the air is much colder.
