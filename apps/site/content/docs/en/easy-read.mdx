## What this step is about

Easy Read is an adaptation of the content into simpler language and structure — shorter sentences, common words, one idea at a time. It supports learners with intellectual disabilities, learners still developing literacy, and anyone who benefits from plainer text.

An Easy Read version is not a summary and not a replacement. It is the same content, made more approachable, offered alongside the original.

## Decisions to make

- **Does your audience need it?** If the ADT will be used in inclusive classrooms, an Easy Read version widens who can participate meaningfully.
- **Review is essential.** Simplifying content is a pedagogical act. Check that the simplified version preserves the meaning and does not talk down to the reader.
