For many deaf learners, sign language is their first language — and written text is a second one. Sign language videos embedded alongside the content make an ADT genuinely accessible to these learners, rather than only technically readable.

ADTs produced with the initiative have included sign language in national sign languages (for example ASL and LIBRAS in existing ADTs).

## Decisions to make

- **Which sign language?** Sign languages are not universal — each country (sometimes each region) has its own. The right choice depends on where the ADT will be used.
- **What is the source of the videos?** Sign language content requires human signers and video production — it is not generated automatically by the tool. This stage is for organizing videos you already have, not producing them.

## Uploading and assigning videos

Sign Language needs your book's sections built first — if [Storyboard](/docs/convert-pdf/storyboard) isn't finished yet, the stage tells you so instead of letting you run it. Once it's ready, open the **Sign Language** stage: there's no AI step to run here, just videos to upload and match to sections.

- **Add videos** to upload one or more `.mp4`/`.webm` files. Uploaded videos start out **unassigned** until you match them to a section.
- **Assign** each video to a Storyboard section from a dropdown — one video per section. A **Coverage** bar shows how many of the book's sections have a video, and a **Next missing section** shortcut jumps straight to the next gap.
- **Manage all sections** opens a full list of every section, so you can review or reassign videos beyond the one-at-a-time flow.

The reader shows the matching video in a small player as the user scrolls through the page.

![The Sign Language stage before any videos are uploaded, showing zero coverage alongside a reader preview prompting for a video](./sign-language-stage.png)

<Callout title="Not part of the AI pipeline">
Sign Language doesn't run or regenerate anything — it's pure asset management. There's nothing to rerun when upstream content changes; if a section's text changes meaningfully, revisit whether its video still matches.
</Callout>
