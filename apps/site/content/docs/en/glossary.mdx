## What this step is about

A glossary helps learners meet new vocabulary with confidence. ADT Studio identifies key terms in your content and generates a **pedagogically aligned glossary** — definitions written to support learning, not just dictionary entries.

## Decisions to make

- **Which terms deserve an entry?** Specialised, technical, or curriculum-relevant vocabulary — yes. Everyday words — usually not. Review the generated selection with your audience in mind.
- **Is the definition at the right level?** A definition written above the reading level of the audience defeats its purpose. Check that the language matches the learners.
