Before enhancing or exporting, take a moment to confirm that what you have is right. This happens in two complementary ways.

## Preview: check it as a reader

The preview shows your ADT as a reader will experience it. Open it and go through the book — not as its producer, but as someone encountering it for the first time.

- Does the content read naturally, in the right order?
- Do images appear where they should?
- Does navigation work — can you move between sections easily?

## Validate: check it against accessibility standards

ADT Studio includes an **accessibility evaluator** that checks your ADT against WCAG, the international accessibility standard. It flags issues before you publish — missing image descriptions, structural problems, contrast issues, and similar.

Accessibility validation is not a formality here. It is the point of the tool. An ADT that fails these checks is not yet doing its job for the learners who depend on it.

## Fix, rerun, repeat

If validation or your own review surfaces problems, go back to the step responsible — [Sectioning](/docs/sectioning) for structure issues, [Storyboard](/docs/storyboard) for layout issues — fix it, and rerun. Only the affected work is redone; everything else is reused.
