## What this step is about

For many deaf learners, sign language is their first language — and written text is a second one. Sign language videos embedded alongside the content make an ADT genuinely accessible to these learners, rather than only technically readable.

ADTs produced with the initiative have included sign language in national sign languages (for example ASL and LIBRAS in existing ADTs).

## Decisions to make

- **Which sign language?** Sign languages are not universal — each country (sometimes each region) has its own. The right choice depends on where the ADT will be used.
- **What is the source of the videos?** Sign language content requires human signers and video production — it is not generated automatically by the tool.
