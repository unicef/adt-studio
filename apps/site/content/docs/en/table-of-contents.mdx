## What this step is about

The table of contents is built automatically from the structure you confirmed during [Sectioning](/docs/sectioning). It gives every reader — and every screen reader — a navigable map of the book.

## Decisions to make

- **Does it reflect the real book?** If something looks wrong here — missing chapters, odd nesting, wrong titles — the cause is almost always in the sectioning, not in the table of contents itself. Go back to [Sectioning](/docs/sectioning), fix the structure, and the table of contents follows.
- **Is the depth right?** A table of contents with every tiny sub-heading can be harder to navigate than one with a clear, shallow structure.
