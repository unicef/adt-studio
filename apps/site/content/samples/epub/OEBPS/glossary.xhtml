Glossary

body

🧍🐾 the whole physical person or animal

broken

🩹💔 damaged so that something is not whole or working properly

cloud

☁️🌥️ a white or gray shape in the sky made of tiny drops of water

crash

💥😵 to hit something very hard

fall

⬇️😨 to move downward through the air to the ground

feather

🪶🐦 one of the soft, light parts that cover a bird's body and wings

fly

🪽☁️ to move through the air

graze
