Auto mode: how it works and what it costs

Engine E3 measures the PDF, reads it twice with AI at the same time, and picks settings with a fixed rulebook. Every number here comes from real runs inside ADT Studio on the 6 PoC books (7 to 12 pages, 4 runs per model), on test books of 12 to 400 pages, and on your 14 books (3 runs per model).

Prices: OpenAI standard tier, USD per 1M tokens: gpt-5.4 $2.50 in / $15 out, gpt-5.4-mini $0.75 in / $4.50 out. Running the same PDF again costs nothing: answers are cached on disk.

How it works

Measure, read twice, apply fixed rules, ask only when needed

The AI never picks settings. It describes the book in plain facts a teacher could check. A fixed rulebook turns those facts into settings, so the same facts always give the same settings. Two readings that look at the book in different ways tell us when a fact is uncertain.

1 · Scan the PDF, no AI

Every page is opened once with the same PDF library ADT already uses. For each page we measure page size, visible text and hidden text (an OCR layer over a scan), where pictures sit and how much of the page they cover, how many drawn lines and shapes it has, and whether words sit on top of a large picture. The edges of facing pages are compared to see if one picture runs across the gutter.

From these numbers come the measured facts: how pages are stored (single pages, two-page openings split in two, or already wide), whether the PDF has real text or is a scan, labelled diagrams (40+ drawn shapes with text on the page), exercise cues (blanks, boxes, numbered items, instruction verbs in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French) and the language.

2 · Choose the pages the AI sees

Books of 30 pages or fewer: the label reading sees every page. The question reading sees up to 6 pages picked from the measurements: the first inside page, the page with the most exercise cues, the most words on pictures, the most drawn shapes, the middle page and the last inside page.

Longer books: a fixed sample of the opening (6% of the book, 10 to 24 pages), 10 pages spread through the middle and the last 4. The question reading uses 6 of the middle pages, so a long foreword can't mislead it. The sample stays at about 40 pages, which is why AI time and cost barely grow with length.

3a · Page-label reading

Each chosen page is sent as a small picture (512 px) with its first 300 characters. The AI labels every page on its own: cover, front matter, story, lesson, exercise, informational, back matter or blank; where the words sit (on the picture, beside it, mostly text, none); and whether it has a meaningful figure such as a map or a labelled diagram.

Book facts are then counted from the labels with fixed thresholds: only pages of the book itself vote, the most common type gives the kind of book, and a count under 60% is flagged. A few odd pages can't drag the whole book.

3b · Plain-question reading

Six pages at 1000 px, their text, and the measured numbers. Four questions in the same plain words the user sees: what is this book mostly; does it ask the reader to write or mark answers (colouring doesn't count); where are the words on a page; are there pictures with labels that explain something. Each answer cites the pages it comes from and gives one sentence a teacher can check.

This reading is the second opinion. Its sentence is shown to the user when it's asked about a fact.

The server sends the settings for all 81 combinations of the four facts, so when the user answers a question the screen updates the picks without running the rules in the browser.

When the user is asked

A fact is put to the user only when both are true:

  • another answer would change a setting for this book, and
  • the two readings disagree, or the label reading saw fewer than two pages of the book itself (for example a PDF that is only a foreword).

Nothing is pre-picked. When the readings disagree their answers are tagged "Our guess" and "Other possibility". The AI's own confidence is not used: in the experiments it flagged none of 7 wrong facts, while disagreement between the two readings caught 5 of 5.

What is kept

  • The book's config gets the confirmed settings, the same way the manual wizard writes them.
  • setup-recommendation.json in the book folder keeps the measured facts, both readings, the questions asked, and every AI prompt and response, so nothing is hidden.
  • AI answers are cached by their exact input, so checking the same PDF again is instant and free.
  • Any provider that accepts images works: the calls go through ADT's own AI client. The model is set by ADT_SETUP_RECOMMENDER_MODEL, or the app's default model.

Book length

A 400-page book takes 6 to 15 seconds on this machine

Long books are read on a fixed sample of about 40 pages, so AI time grows only a little. The scan reads every page and grows with length: 2 to 7 ms per page at 400 pages here, up to about 30 ms per page on picture books whose pages each have their own images.

Test books built by repeating the pages of Egypt (school book, many pictures). Repeated pages share their images, which makes the scan faster than a real book of that length. With unshared images, 150 pages scanned in 4.1 s (27 ms per page), so a real 400-page picture book could need about 11 s of scan.

Network

On a slow connection, sending the pages is the slowest part

Each book sends 0.6 to 2.5 MB of small page pictures to the AI. On a 1 Mbit/s upload that adds 5 to 21 seconds, more than the AI itself. At 20 Mbit/s it is about 1 second.

Money

Cost for many books

400-page books cost more because about 40 pages are read instead of 7 to 12. Output is estimated at 430 tokens per book (measured average 400 to 410).

Tokens

The question reading costs twice the page-label reading

Input tokens are about 80% of the bill. The question reading sends 6 pages at 1000 px; the label reading sends every page of a short book at 512 px. Making the question pictures smaller is the cheapest saving to try, but it has to be checked for accuracy first.

Accuracy at that price

Both models picked every setting right, or asked

Against the PoC answer key, 23 settings per run. "Caught by a question" means the wrong pick depended on a fact the user was asked about. These 6 books were also used to write the rules, so this is an upper bound until the unseen-books test (T1).

What this suggests

Use gpt-5.4-mini for auto mode

  • On these books mini was as accurate as gpt-5.4, twice as fast and a third of the price. Today the route uses the app's default model (gpt-5.4); one line changes that.
  • For slow connections, send smaller page pictures: the question reading at 1000 px is the biggest part of the upload and of the bill. To test for accuracy before changing.
  • The scan is cheap but runs on every page. Saving it into the book and reusing it in Extract (test T6) would pay it back.

Not measured yet

  • A slow laptop: all times are from one desktop machine (WSL).
  • Real long books: the 50 to 400 page books are repeated pages of a short book.
  • Other AI providers (test T4): prices and speed will differ.
  • Time a person spends on the review screen (test T5).
