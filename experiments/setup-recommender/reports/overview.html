Why auto mode is unpredictable, and what we tried

We ran the current recommender and three new approaches on the same six PDFs, three times each, and compared their picks with settings known to work.

1 · The settings

Not every setting is the same kind of question

Before testing, we went through every setting in the code. Three different things decide them, and some of them can never be changed once the book is imported.

Decided by how the PDF is built

Can't change after import

  • Page grouping (single / spread)
  • Figure extraction
  • Image filters and segmentation
  • Watermark removal
  • Page range

Extract can't be re-run on a book with pages. A wrong pick means a new book. Measure these; don't guess them.

Decided by what the book is

Cheap to change later

  • Preset (textbook / storybook / reference)
  • Render strategy
  • Sectioning mode
  • Activities

Needs judgement about content: is it a story, are there exercises, does text sit on the pictures. This is where the AI helps.

Decided by the person

Goals and preferences

  • Languages to publish in
  • Style guide
  • Easy read, speech, glossary

No PDF can answer these. Ask, or keep defaults.

4 · Book by book

What each approach picked

  • Current approach: render strategy is where it fails. On the research paper it said llm, llm-overlay and fixed_layout in three runs. It called the Spanish story a textbook every time.
  • Facts approach: all six misses are one book. The El Viaje excerpt is a novel's prologue, so from those pages "reference" is a fair answer. One user correction ("It's a story") fixes every setting.
  • Page grouping by measurement: right on every book, every time. The spread detector ADT already has beats asking the AI.

6 · How far to trust this

Promising, not proven

  • Small set: 4 labelled books plus 2 without full labels, and the PDFs are 7–12 page excerpts.
  • In-sample rules: the rules and thresholds were written while looking at these same books. E1's perfect score is a fit, not evidence. E2 shares those rules.
  • Simulated descriptions: the E3 one-liners were written by us.
  • Next: run every approach unchanged on your converted books. A second agent is now trying more approaches (page-by-page voting, one call instead of four, a simulated confirm chat, text-only for providers without images) and will report back.
